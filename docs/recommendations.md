# Recommendations

## 90-Day Improvement Roadmap

### 1. Carrier appointment discipline
Use appointment windows and exception review to reduce waiting time and improve dock turnaround. Track carrier arrival punctuality and late-arrival reasons.

### 2. Wave-to-departure synchronization
Release warehouse waves according to transport departure priorities so that picking and staging are completed close to the required loading time without creating congestion.

### 3. Transport load consolidation
Combine compatible orders by destination, delivery window and vehicle capacity where feasible. Monitor truck utilization together with OTIF to avoid service deterioration.

### 4. Inventory exception management
Review repeated stock-outs by SKU-location and classify root causes such as forecast error, replenishment delay, master-data issue or execution failure.

### 5. Data-quality controls
Validate order dates, goods-issue times, delivery confirmation, shipment quantities, inventory records and unit-of-measure consistency before KPI calculation.

### 6. KPI ownership
Assign one accountable owner to each KPI. A poor result should generate an action, due date and follow-up rather than remaining only as a dashboard indicator.

## Prioritization Logic

Improvements can be ranked by:

`Priority Score = Business Impact × Urgency × Controllability`

High-priority actions are those that materially affect customer service or cost, require near-term intervention and can be influenced by the logistics team.

## Recommended Review Cadence

- **Daily:** critical transport, dock, picking and stock-out exceptions.
- **Weekly:** OTIF, truck utilization, pick accuracy, dock turnaround and stock-out root causes.
- **Monthly:** full scorecard, inventory turnover, freight cost, composite performance score and management action review.
- **Quarterly:** KPI definitions, target levels, weights and reporting architecture.

## Expected Outcome

The objective is not to maximize every KPI independently. The framework aims to improve service and execution while maintaining reasonable transport cost, inventory exposure and operational stability.
